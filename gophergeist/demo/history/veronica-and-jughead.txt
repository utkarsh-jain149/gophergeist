Veronica and Jughead

Gopherspace was a maze with no map, so people built search engines to find
their way through the tunnels.

Veronica appeared in 1992, written by Steven Foster and Fred Barrie at the
University of Nevada, Reno. The name is a backronym: Very Easy
Rodent-Oriented Net-wide Index to Computerized Archives. It was a nod to
Archie, the earlier search engine for FTP archives, since Veronica is a
character in the Archie comics.

Veronica crawled the menus of Gopher servers around the world, night after
night. You reached her through a search item on a menu: you typed some words,
and she answered with a new menu of matching items that you could follow
straight into the dark.

Jughead followed in 1993, written by Rhett "Jonzy" Jones at the University of
Utah. Its backronym was Jonzy's Universal Gopher Hierarchy Excavation And
Display. Jughead searched one server or a small group of servers rather than
all of Gopherspace, the way a lantern lights one room instead of the house.

A descendant called Veronica-2 is still run today by the Floodgap gopher
server. So the name never fully died. Some say she is still crawling.
