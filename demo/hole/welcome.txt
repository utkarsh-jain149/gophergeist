Welcome, traveler

You have tunneled into a gopherhole. Before the web won, this is how a lot of
the internet looked: plain menus, plain text, and nothing that moved.

This hole is haunted. Pick "Ask Veronica" on the main menu and type a
question. Veronica was Gopherspace's search engine in 1992. She has been
asleep for a long time, but a small open-weight model running on this very
machine lends her a voice. She only answers from the documents in this hole,
and she tells you which ones she read.

Look around:

  history/   how Gopher was born, and how it died
  ofrenda/   an altar for other protocols the internet left behind

Leave the lights off. Nothing here needs the internet.
