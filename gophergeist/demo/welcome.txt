Welcome, traveler

You have tunneled into a gopherhole, and something down here noticed.

Before the web won, this is how a lot of the internet looked: plain menus,
plain text, nothing that moved. Most of those servers went dark long ago.
This one never quite shut down. On quiet nights the menus still flicker.

Pick "Ask Veronica" on the main menu and type a question. Veronica was
Gopherspace's search engine in 1992. She has been asleep for a very long
time. A small open-weight model running on this very machine lends her a
voice, but she only speaks of what is written in these files, and she always
tells you which ones she read. Ask her about anything else and she will tell
you the archives are silent.

Wander if you dare:

  history/   how Gopher was born, and how it died
  ofrenda/   an altar for the other protocols the internet buried

Leave the lights off. Nothing here needs the internet. Nothing here ever left.
