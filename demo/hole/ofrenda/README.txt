An ofrenda for dead protocols

For Dia de los Muertos, families build ofrendas: altars with photos,
marigolds, candles and the favorite foods of people they have lost, so the
dead can find their way home for a night.

This folder is an ofrenda for the protocols the internet left behind. Each
file remembers one of them: who made it, what it was for, and what it gave
to the things that came after. Light a candle for them. Most of what we use
today stands on their shoulders.
