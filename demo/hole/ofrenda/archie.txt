Archie (1990)

Archie is usually called the first internet search engine. It was created in
1990 at McGill University in Montreal by Alan Emtage, with Bill Heelan and
Peter Deutsch. The name comes from "archive" with the v taken out.

Archie regularly collected the file listings of public FTP servers and let you
search them by file name. If you knew a program was called something, Archie
told you which servers had it. It did not look inside files, only at names.

What it gave us: the idea of crawling many servers and building one index.
Veronica and Jughead, Gopher's search engines, took their names from Archie's
comic book friends.

Favorite food on the altar: a long directory listing, served cold.
